Our Story

Beauty with purpose

Luxe Era Salon started with a simple idea: a salon where everyone feels welcome and leaves feeling their best. Over 8 years we have grown into a trusted name for hair, skin, nails, bridal and men's grooming.

Our stylists train regularly on the latest techniques, and we only use professional, skin-safe products. Whether it is a quick trim or a full makeover, you get our complete attention.

  • Personal consultation before every service
  • Strict hygiene and sanitised tools
  • Transparent pricing, no surprises
