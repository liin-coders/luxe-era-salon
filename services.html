FAQ

Service questions

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead guarantees your preferred stylist and time.

How long does a colour service take?

Global colour takes around 2 hours; balayage and highlights can take 2–3 hours depending on hair length.

Are the prices final?

Prices shown are starting prices. The final amount depends on hair length, type and products, and is confirmed at consultation.

Do you use branded products?

Yes, we use professional, skin-safe products and will always tell you what is being used on you.
